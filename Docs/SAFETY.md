# Child safety requirements

- Never gate core story progression or rewards on eating or tasting.
- Always offer decline, skip, pause, and story-only modes.
- No bite quotas, weight tracking, or coercive prompts.
- Adult supervision required during meals; avoid distracted eating.
- Parent chooses age-appropriate food preparation and restrictions.
- Minimize child data; no automatic camera/microphone monitoring.
- Professional feeding and children's privacy review before release.
