# Eat Your Veggies 🥦

Parent-guided Unity 3D adventure for ages 2–5. Offline-first, food-positive, and designed to make vegetable discovery optional and playful.

## Current status
Preproduction / foundation. **Not yet compiled or playable.**

## First vertical slice
Broccoli Forest, avatar customization, parent setup, guided quest, optional food discovery, and a calm adventure HUD.

## Principles
- No eating required for progress or rewards.
- No child-facing ads, purchasing prompts, or pressure.
- Adult-supervised food activities; parental safety choices.
- Local-first profiles and minimal child data.

See Docs/ACTIVE_ISSUES.md, Docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, and Docs/SAFETY.md.
